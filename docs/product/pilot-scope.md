# Synthetic pilot scope

## Identity

- Jurisdiction: Harborfield Pilot District, fictional.
- Organization: Harborfield Civic Works Lab, fictional.
- Product label: Civic Reporting — synthetic pilot.
- Delivery area and all sample records must remain obviously synthetic.

The public interface must always say that it is not a real government service.
It must direct urgent danger away from this non-emergency demonstration without
inventing a real local emergency number.

## Phase 1

Phase 1 establishes the pnpm workspace, Next.js application, design tokens,
public shell, reporting shell, staff and moderation shells, narrow platform
backstop, public information routes, test harnesses, and representative loading,
empty, permission, offline, stale, network-error, and recovery states.

It does not implement Supabase, authentication, persisted reporting, media
processing, staff actions, delivery, notifications, or production operations.

## Pilot promises

Later phases may make only evidence-backed statements:

- accepted by the platform is not delivered;
- delivered is not acknowledged;
- organization-marked resolution remains actor-qualified;
- exact location and verified contact stay restricted;
- the list and structured address route work without a map;
- public content appears only after its publication gate.
