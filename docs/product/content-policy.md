# Synthetic content and media policy

Only safe, synthetic fixtures may be used in local tests and demonstrations.
Never add actual sexual, graphic, illegal, identifying, or distressing material.

Public image upload and publication are disabled by default. A later technical
path must remain fail-closed unless an approved moderation provider and every
legal, privacy, staffing, retention, reviewer-safety, cost, latency, and incident
gate are configured. Local tests may select an explicit fake moderation adapter.

Original media, filenames, raw storage paths, OCR, moderation signals, internal
notes, exact restricted locations, reporter contact, and legal-case information
must not appear in public HTML, APIs, maps, notifications, logs, metadata, social
previews, or cacheable URLs.

Text, deterministic title inputs, staff public updates, notification previews,
and derived content also require publication checks in later phases. Phase 1
ships explanatory shells only and makes no moderation capability claim.
